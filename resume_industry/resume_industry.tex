\section{Selected Quantitative \& Research Projects}
\entry{\href{https://aminshz.github.io/my-website/paper1.pdf}{Structural Modeling of Household Financial Behavior} — Job Market Paper}{2022--Present}
\begin{itemize}
\item Built and calibrated a heterogeneous-agent structural model of household saving to U.S. Health and Retirement Study (HRS) microdata; solved the dynamic optimization problem numerically in C++/Python.
